Privacy Policy

Overview

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the MyOrtho Companion – Knee mobile application (the “App,” shown on your home screen as “My Knee”) collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. The App is published by MyOrtho Solutions (“we,” “us,” or “our”). The App provides general education and personal tracking tools for people considering or recovering from knee replacement. It does not provide medical care.

Information That Stays on Your Device

The information you enter in the App, including your name (if you provide one), surgery and appointment dates, treatments, journal entries, check-ins, activity and physical therapy tracking, checklists, and achievements, is stored only on your iPhone. We do not receive it, and it is not sent to any server. If you delete the App, this information is deleted with it, except where it is included in your own device or iCloud backups.

Asking questions in the guide (AI)

When you type a question into the Knee Replacement Guide, the App sends the question, the recent messages in that conversation, and a random identifier created when the App was installed to our server at snap-ortho.com. Our server sends the question and conversation to Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI model), which generates the answer. The random identifier is used only to limit how many questions can be sent per minute; it does not identify you by name.

We do not store your questions in a database. Our hosting provider keeps routine technical request logs, and Anthropic processes and may temporarily retain the text under its API data policies. Please do not include your name, contact details, or other personal information in your questions. Questions you type can describe your health, so use this feature only if you are comfortable with them being processed as described here.

Advertising Measurement (Meta and Branch)

The App includes software from Meta Platforms (Facebook) and Branch Metrics so we can measure whether our advertising leads people to install and open the App. When you first set up the App, iOS asks whether the App may track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

  • If you allow tracking, the App shares limited information with Meta and Branch: that the App was installed and opened, your device’s advertising identifier, device and operating system details, IP address, and time of use. Because these companies know this information comes from a knee replacement app, it may suggest that you, or someone you care for, are interested in knee treatment. Meta and Branch use this information under their own privacy policies.
  • If you do not allow tracking, or have not answered yet, the App does not start Meta’s software, and Branch is limited to opening links to the App without measuring advertising.

The App never sends your journal, dates, trackers, or other health entries to Meta or Branch. You can change your choice at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Versions of the App earlier than 1.22 started Meta’s and Branch’s software when the App opened, before asking about tracking. In those versions, these companies could receive the install, app-open, device, and IP information described above even if you declined tracking, although not your advertising identifier. Updating to the latest version applies the choice described above.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the App asks iOS for permission to send notifications. Reminders for your appointments, surgery preparation, exercises, and check-ins are scheduled on your iPhone by the App itself. They are not sent through our servers or any push notification service, and we do not receive your reminder times or their contents.

Reminder text can appear on your Lock Screen and may mention appointments or your surgery. To keep details private, turn off “Show details on Lock Screen” in the App’s Reminders settings, or change notification previews in the iPhone Settings app. You can turn reminders off at any time in the App or in Settings › Notifications.

Calendar

If you choose to export dates to Apple Calendar, the App asks for permission to add events. It can only add events; it cannot read your existing calendar. Exported events, including any alerts the App adds to them, are stored in your calendar, not by us.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell your information. We do not create accounts, and we do not collect your email address, phone number, or location through the App. We do not use the information you enter in the App for advertising.

Children

The App is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Choices and Rights

You can decline or turn off tracking at any time in iOS Settings, turn reminders off, avoid using the guide's “Ask a question” feature, and delete all information stored by the App by deleting the App. Depending on where you live (for example, Washington, Nevada, Connecticut, California, or the European Union), you may have rights to request access to, deletion of, or information about personal or consumer health data. Because we do not keep App data on our servers, most requests can be met by deleting the App; for anything else, contact us using the details below. To ask Meta or Branch about information they hold, contact them directly.

Security

We implement commercially reasonable security measures designed to safeguard data under our control. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and as such, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy
