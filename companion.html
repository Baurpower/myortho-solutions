😟

Conflicting advice

Lost motivation

Setbacks & reoperations

Questions families ask

Is this a replacement for my parent's doctor or physical therapist? +
No — and it's not meant to be. MyOrtho Companion works alongside the care team, helping your parent follow their plan, stay motivated, and share progress. Always follow the guidance of your surgeon and therapist.
My parent isn't very tech-savvy. Can they use it? +
Yes. The app is built to be simple — large tap targets, plain language, and one clear thing to do each day. Many families set it up together, then the patient uses it on their own.
Is their health information private? +
Privacy is central to the design. See our privacy policy for the details.
What does it cost? +
MyOrtho Companion is a one-time purchase on the App Store — no subscription. Tap "Download on the App Store" to see current pricing.
Which devices are supported? +
MyOrtho Companion is available for iPhone on the Apple App Store.
