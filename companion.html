Recovery is hard to manage from the sidelines

Recovery can feel overwhelming

When a parent has surgery, families are left to fill the gaps. The result is stress for everyone and recoveries that stall.

Between appointments you are often on your own, unsure what is normal or what to do next. That uncertainty is where recoveries stall.

😟

Conflicting advice

The surgeon says one thing, the therapist another, and the internet a hundred more. It is hard to know what to actually do today.

📉

Lost motivation

Progress feels invisible. Without small wins to track, it is easy to get discouraged and skip the movement that matters most.

🔁

Setbacks and reoperations

A confusing, fragmented recovery is a risky one. Missed rehab can lead to stiffness, and sometimes another trip to the OR.
